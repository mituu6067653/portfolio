## 3. Design System & Rules Baseline
- **Design Style:** Editorial + Modern Product Design (Quiet confidence).
- **Color Tokens:**
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A` | Text Secondary: `#475569`
  - Background: `#F8FAFC` | Surface: `#FFFFFF` | Border: `#E2E8F0`
  - Accent: `#2563EB` | Accent Hover: `#1D4ED8` | Success: `#16A34A`
- **Typography:** Inter (or Manrope/system sans-serif).
- **Services Offered:**
  1. Business Websites
  2. Custom Web Applications
  3. Website Redesign
  4. Bug Fixes & Improvements
- **Process Steps:** `01 Understand → 02 Plan → 03 Build → 04 Launch`
- **Rules & Guardrails:**
  - Never invent clients, revenue, fake stats, or fake testimonials.
  - Keep client components minimal; prefer SSR/Static.
  - Server-side validation required for contact form; no exposed credentials.
  - Micro-animations only; support reduced-motion.
